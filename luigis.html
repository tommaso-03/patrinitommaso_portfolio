luigi's / Branding / 2026

Luigi’s Film Lab is an emerging analog film development and scanning service currently taking shape within the photography scene. Founded by Lino Luigi Faglia, a young engineer and designer driven by curiosity and positive energy, the project is born out of a deep passion for the analog medium. Rather than just a standard technical service, Luigi’s aims to be an informal community hub where the thoughtful art of shooting film meets the Italian ritual of sharing a good coffee. It serves as an open meeting point for both seasoned photographers and beginners to connect, exchange advice, and talk about photography in a relaxed environment.

Operating on a highly personal scale, the lab provides processing and scanning with a strong focus on precision. The philosophy centers on treating every roll with the exact chemistry it needs, while tailoring the results to reflect the photographer's unique vision. By keeping the process simple and completely free of intimidating technical jargon, Luigi’s acts as a reliable partner for amateur shooters. Driven by the motto "For the Love of Film," the lab takes time for each person, ensuring that expert guidance and a friendly chat are always available.
