William Eggleston / Web development / 2026

William Eggleston is a pioneering American photographer known for his vivid color images that capture the mundane and the everyday with a unique artistic vision. His work has had a profound influence not only on the acceptance of color photography as a legitimate art form, but also on my photography style and approach.

I worked on the UI of the website, developing it in HTML + CSS + JS in one week, to create an engaging online portfolio that showcases Eggleston's groundbreaking selected work.
